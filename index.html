FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Find quick answers to common questions about ISO Certification.

1. How long does it take to get ISO Certified?
Depending on company size and standard required, certification can be completed in as fast as 3 to 7 working days after documentation completion.
2. What is the validity of an ISO Certificate?
Most ISO certificates are valid for 3 years, subject to successful annual surveillance audits conducted in the 1st and 2nd years.
3. Is ISO Certification mandatory for government tenders?
Yes, most government tenders and MNC vendor registrations strictly require ISO 9001 or standard-specific certifications as a eligibility criteria.
4. Can small businesses or startups get ISO Certified?
Absolutely! ISO standards apply to organizations of all scales, from single-person startups to enterprise corporations.
